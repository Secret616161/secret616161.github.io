Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

CrownHunters Studio is a private tool used only by the CrownHunters team. It connects to the team's own TikTok account through the official TikTok API.

What we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok account (display name, account ID) to confirm the connection.
  • Upload of our own gameplay videos as drafts to our own account.
  • Public statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments count, shares) to see which videos work best.

What we do not do

  • We do not access other people's TikTok accounts.
  • We do not collect, store or sell personal data of viewers or players.
  • We do not read or store individual comments or user profiles.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

Storage

Access tokens and video statistics are stored only on the team's own computer and can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings.

Children

The tool is not used by or directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: send a message to our TikTok account or open an issue in this website's GitHub repository.
